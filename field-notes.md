## Students on learning & struggling (all levels)
- **Lyla Panichas, 78** — real learner in a Cyber-Seniors/digiAGE program (got her first iPad 3 months before the interview). Quote: "The first time my tutor called me, I mean, the kids rattle things off so fast. I said, Wait a minute. You have a little old lady here. Let me keep up with you. I couldn't keep up and I ended up crying." She persisted — next week she started grasping it; now she plays games online, streams movies, and has Zoom get-togethers with her son and sister: "It's kind of lifted my fears of being isolated." Source: https://kffhealthnews.org/aging/calming-computer-jitters-help-for-seniors-who-arent-tech-savvy/ — Why it matters: patience is a feature — LiqLearns' tutor-until-understood contract is exactly what turned her from tears to independence; potential TikTok topic on teaching older learners.
